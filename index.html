Certify.mn — Blockchain Diploma Verification

TEO Blockchain

0 → 1 Launch Government Adopted Blockchain · EdTech

Problem

Mongolia's Ministry of Education discovered 724 fraudulent and 2,000 suspicious diplomas out of 59,000 checked in 2022. Fake credentials were slipping into government jobs and classrooms.

For legitimate graduates, verifying a paper diploma meant expensive notarization and weeks of delays — a real barrier for anyone applying to jobs abroad or continuing education overseas.

My Approach

I named the product, owned go-to-market strategy, and defined the pricing model. The core insight: this couldn't just be a tech solution — the Ministry and universities had to adopt it simultaneously, or it would fail on both sides.

I overruled internal pressure for a feature-heavy v1 in favor of a drag-and-drop MVP. I ran blockchain education sessions for institution stakeholders with zero prior familiarity, then took the beta directly to both government officials and university administrators — walking each group through the same platform. Any HR manager or employer can now verify a diploma instantly by drag-and-dropping the file, with no notary, no calls, no waiting.

Outcome

Beta launched in 3 months with a 4-person team. After a second 3-month iteration based on real institution feedback, the platform was officially adopted by Mongolia's General Education Authority — now mandated under Mongolian law for all university graduates.

75 institutions onboarded. 60,000+ credentials issued to date. Verification went from weeks and notary fees to instant and free.

NIKO AI Scoring — No-Code ML for Banks

AND Solutions · nikoscoring.ai

0 → 1 Launch B2B SaaS · SEA AI · Fintech

Problem

Banks and lending institutions across Southeast Asia were making credit decisions with spreadsheets and gut instinct. Building proper ML credit scoring models required a data science team — something most regional lenders couldn't afford or hire.

AND Solutions needed a new product it could sell into the SEA market. There was no internal roadmap, no validated concept — just a gap in the market and an opportunity to own it.

My Approach

I identified the opportunity through 100+ outbound discovery conversations with banks and lenders across Southeast Asia, built the business case, and presented it to the steering committee — getting sign-off to build from scratch. I named it NIKO, after my own name.

Working directly with ML engineers, I defined the product: a no-code AutoML platform where banks could upload their data, train predictive models, and deploy them without writing a single line of code. I created an ELI5 explainer video to make ML concepts accessible to non-technical bank executives, and personally ran client demos across the region.

Outcome

NIKO became the second product AND Solutions ever sold into the Southeast Asian market. It's now a live SaaS platform used by banks, insurance companies, and lenders for credit scoring, debt collection, churn prediction, and insurance claim modeling.

LendMN — Gamification to Reduce Default Risk

AND Solutions · lend.mn

Behavioral Design Fintech · Mobile Data-Driven

Problem

During a quarterly review, I noticed the risk team was flagging a quiet but dangerous trend — early repayers were shifting to on-time, and on-time payers were slipping 1–2 days late. No penalty existed for the first 3 days, so nothing was catching it.

I proactively got sign-off to investigate before it materially hit portfolio quality. Analyzing 12 months of data from active users, I confirmed the behavioral shift was real and accelerating across both segments.

My Approach

I ran structured customer interviews — 5 interviews with ML engineers and the CS team — testing multiple hypotheses: financial hardship, notification gaps, payment friction. All ruled out. The actual root cause: customers had no reason to pay early.

Gamification emerged from a cross-team brainstorm, not as my first instinct. We skipped reminders and nudges because the problem wasn't awareness — it was motivation. I designed a bonus points system tied to things customers cared about: higher loan limits and reduced fees. Presented to the steering committee, approved, and launched within 3 months.

Outcome

We targeted 10% improvement in early repayments. Customers adopted far faster than projected — we hit 18%. That created a new problem: points cost exceeded budget. So we iterated: dialed back points per repayment, but expanded redemption value across BNPL and installment products.

What started as a repayment fix became a cross-product loyalty system. Default risk dropped 5%, meaningfully improving portfolio quality.
